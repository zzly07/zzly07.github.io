Game Designer & Developer

Zhilingyun Zhang

I aim at creating interactive designs with a playful mindset, and exploring new paths through applying cross-disciplinary ideas and inspirations into game design.

I’m currently pursuing an MFA degree at University of Southern California in Interactive Media and Game Design. I hold a bachelor’s degree in History and Economics and a minor in Game Studies and Design from University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign.
