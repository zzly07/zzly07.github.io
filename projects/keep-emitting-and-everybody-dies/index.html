00 / OVERVIEWSIDE A

Overview

Keep Emitting and EVERYBODY Dies! is a personal board game project developed from August to November 2024. It translates the economic idea of cap and trade into a conflict between individual profit and collective survival.

Players compete for carbon credits through auctions and manage factory production, while a shared Global Pollution meter ties their interests together. The design asks how players pursue their own advantage when the environmental cost can end the game for everyone.

01 / GAME RULES↘

At a glance & components

Play as factory owners balancing production, emissions, and profit. The government holds annual auctions for emission allowances, which factories need when completing orders. Players can also trade allowances with one another.

3–5 players · Ages 10+ · 30–60 minutes

The game lasts up to 3 years, with 12 months per year: at most 36 monthly turns. Each player has one action per month.

In the box

  • 1 game board.
  • 5 auction forms.
  • Resource tokens in 5 types: 45 allowance tokens and 20 tokens each of iron, water, coal, and sulfur.
  • Coins.
  • 60 order cards.
  • 1 Pollution Level token.
  • 1 Pollution Indicator.
  • 2 Time Indicators.
02 / GAME RULES↘

Set up the table

Before the first month

  1. Place the game board in the center of the table.
  2. Set the Pollution Level to 1 and the Pollution Indicator to 0.
  3. Place the Time Indicators on Year 1 and Month 1.
  4. Put all resource tokens in the bag beside the board.
  5. Separate order cards into Level I, II, and III decks. Reveal 4 cards from each deck, creating 12 available orders.
  6. Give each player $50 in coins.
  7. Draw 3 random resource tokens to form one set. Repeat until 4 sets of 3 tokens are available on the table.
03 / GAME RULES↘

One action each month

At the start of every year, each player receives 5 free allowances, and the government holds an allowance auction. During each of the year’s 12 months, every player chooses one of the three actions below.

Auctions and trading are free actions

Participating in an allowance auction or trading with another player does not consume your monthly action.

A · Acquire resources

  1. Take one of the four resource sets from the table.
  2. Draw a new set of 3 random tokens from the bag to replace it.

B · Complete an order

  1. Return the resources required by the order card to the resource bag, applying any permanent discounts from your completed orders.
  2. Move the Pollution Indicator one space to the right.
  3. Place the completed order in front of you. Its rewards include cash and any resource-discount bonus shown on the card.

C · Protect the environment

  1. Pay the cost for the current Pollution Level, shown in the pollution section below.
  2. Move the Pollution Indicator one space to the left. At 0, it does not move; this action cannot lower the Pollution Level.
  3. Take an available order card and place it horizontally to reserve it. Only you may complete that order.

Resource supply

  • Always keep 4 sets of 3 random resource tokens available on the table.
  • Players keep their remaining ordinary resource tokens at the end of each year.

Allowances are a special resource

  • Every order requires allowances. They are available through the annual allowance distribution and auction, and through trades with other players.
  • At the end of the year, all unused allowances are cleared and returned for re-auction. They do not carry over like ordinary resources.
05 / GAME RULES↘

Pollution & environmental protection

Track pollution with two markers: the Pollution Level and the Pollution Indicator. The level runs from 0 to 3 during play; reaching Level 4 ends the game immediately.

How pollution rises

  • Every completed order advances the Pollution Indicator one space.
  • After 10 spaces, increase the Pollution Level by one and reset the indicator to 0.
  • Each Pollution Level reduces the number of allowances available at the annual auction by 3.

The level cannot be reversed

Protecting the environment moves the Pollution Indicator back by one space and lets you reserve an order. It cannot reduce the Pollution Level. If the indicator is already at 0, the action has no effect on pollution.

06 / GAME RULES↘

The annual allowance auction

The auction follows a cap-and-trade format: players state the highest price they will pay and the number of allowances they want. All winning bidders pay the same clearing price.

Annual supply

Each player receives 5 free allowance tokens at the start of the year. The auction starts from a base pool of 20 allowances, reduced by 3 for each Pollution Level.

Resolve the auction

  1. Each player writes a price and quantity on their auction form.
  2. Reveal all bids simultaneously.
  3. Rank bids from highest price to lowest price, allocating allowances in that order until the available supply has been assigned.
  4. The price of the last winning bid becomes the clearing price. Every winner pays that price per allowance, rather than their own bid price.
  5. Distribute the allowances and pay the costs to the bank.
07 / GAME RULES↘

Trade with other factories

Players may trade coins, resource tokens, and allowances with one another at any time during the game. Trading does not consume an action.

08 / GAME RULES↘

Orders, rewards & permanent bonuses

Orders come in three levels. Higher-level cards require more resources and offer greater rewards. Each card shows its level, resource requirements, and rewards.

Available and reserved orders

  • Keep the three decks separate, with 4 face-up cards from each level.
  • Whenever a player takes an order card, immediately replace it with a card from the corresponding deck.
  • An order reserved through the Protect Environment action is placed horizontally and can only be completed by the player who reserved it.

Build permanent production discounts

Completed order cards award cash and may provide a permanent discount on the resources needed for future orders. These bonuses are cumulative: discounts from completed cards stack.

09 / GAME RULESEND OF RECORD

When the game ends

Pollution Level 4: everyone loses

If the Pollution Level reaches 4, the game ends immediately and all players lose.

Otherwise, score at the end of Year 3

  1. Add the points on all your completed order cards: Level I = 1, Level II = 2, and Level III = 4.
  2. Add the amount of cash you still hold.
  3. The player with the highest combined total wins.

Final score

Completed-order points + remaining cash = final score.
